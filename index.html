How it works

Three questions every parent has about their gear, answered in order

We start with what it's worth today, because that's the question with no good answer anywhere else right now.

1

What's it worth?

A real resale estimate for the exact item you own — model, condition, and how long you've had it — pulled from actual sold listings, not a guess.

2

When will they outgrow it?

The projected date your child ages out of it, sized to their own growth — because the countdown is exactly why the value question is timely, not academic.

3

What's next?

A neutral recommendation for what comes after — new or secondhand — with no incentive to push you toward either one.

We don't buy your gear. We don't sell it. We just tell you what it's worth.

TotWorth is channel-neutral

Resale marketplaces price your gear to buy it from you at a margin. We don't have inventory, so we don't have a reason to push the number in either direction — the valuation is the product, not a step toward a transaction.
